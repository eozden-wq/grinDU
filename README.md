# GrinDU

Durham University has a fairly large campus with 17 colleges and faculties all over the town. For this reason, it may be quite difficult for students to be able to meet people outside of their comfort circle in their courses. GrinDU is for Durham students to create study groups and meet with random people in their course outside of lectures and the library to study.
